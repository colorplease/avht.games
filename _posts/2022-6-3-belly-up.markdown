# [Belly Up](https://colorplease.github.io/belly-up-web/)

“2D Movement Based Arcade Fish Shooter”

Inspired by *ULTRAKILL and Last Man Sitting*

I sought to make a **challenging** game that was **easy to learn** but **difficult to master** and **rewards players for honing their skills**.


![Image](/avht.games/images/belly up/ezgif-3-b7de0f3b4c.gif)

A game made for the [Mini Jam 109: Depth(s)](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jam-109-depths). Belly Up is a 2D Movement Based Arcade shooter, where you carefully manage your energy levels and momentum to carefully tread down to the bottom of the sea. All sorts of life lurk down in the murky depths, and it takes a skillful pilot to make it all the way down, without going belly up.
